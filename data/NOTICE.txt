N5 vocabulary (data/n5-vocab.json)
  Word ids are the JLPT Vocab Quest N5 indexes (0–717), from the list embedded in
  https://github.com/Ellisdeeman/n5-vocab-quest
  That list follows the N5 deck at
  https://github.com/jamsinclair/open-anki-jlpt-decks (MIT, Copyright (c) 2020 Jamie Sinclair).
  English glosses derive from JMdict (EDRDG, CC BY-SA 4.0): https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html

Kana and kanji charts (data/kana.json, data/kanji.json)
  Original to this game. Kanji are the JLPT N5 set, keyed by the character, which is
  the same key JLPT Vocab Quest uses for kanji. Readings are the common N5 on/kun forms.
