How a turn works
  1. Speed fills a turn gauge. Whoever fills it acts next.
  2. Move along the gold tiles, then attack, use an ability, or wait.
  3. Basic attacks always connect. Abilities ask for a reading: tap a choice, or type romaji or kana.
  4. Right within 4 seconds is a critical hit. A slower correct answer is half strength. Wrong, or 30 seconds of silence, and the action fizzles.
  5. Clear the map to unlock the next battle. A daily fight uses today's weak words.
