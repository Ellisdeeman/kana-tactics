カナ・タクティクス Kana Tactics

A one-map tactics battle for JLPT N5. Move, strike, and cast — abilities land only if you can read the Japanese.

レン · Squire

Hiragana strikes, plus hiragana-only N5 words. 切り込み and 号令.

ミナ · Chemist

Katakana and loanword items such as コーヒー and パン. Potions and tosses.

ソウ · Black Mage

N5 kanji such as 火, 水, and 木, plus kanji words. Fire, water, and a chant.

How a turn works
  1. Speed fills a turn gauge. Whoever fills it acts next.
  2. Move along the gold tiles, then attack, use an ability, or wait.
  3. Basic attacks always connect. Abilities ask for a reading: tap a choice, or type romaji or kana.
  4. Right within 4 seconds is a critical hit. A slower correct answer is half strength. Wrong, or 30 seconds of silence, and the action fizzles.
  5. Win by defeating the goblin, the archer, and the imp.
